# DialDay Support

Need help with DialDay? We're here to help.

## Email Support

Email **petros.canon@gmail.com** for any questions, bug reports, feature requests, or account issues.

Response time: typically within 48 hours.

## What to include in your email

To help us respond faster, please include:

- Your DialDay account email
- A description of what you were trying to do
- What happened instead
- Your device (iPhone model, iOS version)
- Screenshots if relevant

## Account & Subscription Help

- **To cancel a subscription:** open the App Store app on your device, tap your profile picture, tap Subscriptions, and manage DialDay there.
- **Refunds:** managed by Apple. Request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.
- **Account deletion:** in-app via Settings → Account → Delete Account, or email us.
